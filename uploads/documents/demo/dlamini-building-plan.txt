DLAMINI FAMILY RESIDENCE - APPROVED BUILDING PLAN
================================================
Site: 12 Acacia Street, Polokwane
Owner: Dlamini Family Trust
Architect: Studio M Architects (plan no. SM-2026-0114)
Status: APPROVED by Polokwane Municipality - 14 May 2026

Summary of plan:
- Double storey family residence, 340 m2 floor area
- Ground floor: entrance, lounge, kitchen, dining, guest bedroom
- First floor: 3 bedrooms, bathrooms, study
- Reinforced concrete foundations and ring beams
- Pitched roof with steel trusses
